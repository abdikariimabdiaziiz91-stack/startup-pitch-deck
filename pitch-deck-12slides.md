# SpendWise - Startup Pitch Deck (12 Slides)

## Slide 1: Title Slide
- **Startup Name:** SpendWise Dashboard
- **Tagline:** Xalka casriga ah ee maareynta miisaaniyadda iyo la socodka kharashka shakhsiga ah.
- **Founder:** Abdikariim Abdiaziiz Mohamed

## Slide 2: Problem
- Dad badan ayaa dhibaato ku qaba in ay la socdaan halka ay maraan lacagularankooda maalinlaha ah, taas oo keenta in aysan kaydsan karin lacag ama ay ku dhacaan deymo aan la qorsheyn. Ma jiro qalab fudud oo af-Soomaali ama deegaanka ah oo kuu sahlayaya in aad isla markiiba aragto miisaaniyaddaada.

## Slide 3: Solution
- **SpendWise** waa barnaamij web ah oo fudud, kaas oo qof walba u sahlayaya in uu si dhakhso ah u geliyo dakhligiisa iyo kharashkiisa, isla markaana si toos ah u xisaabiya lacagta u hartay iyo halka ay miisaaniyaddiisu marayso.

## Slide 4: Market Opportunity
- Suuqa bartilmaameedka ah waa ardayda, dhalinyarada shaqeysa, iyo ganacsatada yar yar ee u baahan qalab degdeg ah oo digital ah si ay lacagtooda u maamulaan iyaga oo aan la kulmin dhibaatada barnaamijyada bangiyada ee adag.

## Slide 5: Product / Service
- **SpendWise Dashboard:** Waa app ay ku dhex jiraan CSS Grid iyo Flexbox layouts, foomam laga buuxiyo dakhliga iyo kharashka, iyo qayb si toos ah u soo saarta inta ka hartay miisaaniyadda (Remaining Balance Calculator) iyadoo isticmaalaysa JavaScript app logic.

## Slide 6: Business Model
- **Qaabka Dakhliga:** Isticmaalka aasaasiga ah waa bilaash (Free Tier), laakiin waxaa jiro qorshe horumarsan (Premium Plan) oo ganacsiyada iyo dadka u baahan warbixino qoto dheer (Advanced analytics) laga qaado lacag bille ah oo yar (Subscription).

## Slide 7: Market Strategy / Go-To-Market
- Soo bandhigidda app-ka iyada oo la isticmaalayo shabakadaha bulshada, la shaqaynta dhalinyarada tignoolajiyada ku jirta (PLP community), iyo soo bandhigidda tusaalooyin fudud oo muujinaya sida miisaaniyadda loo habeeyo.

## Slide 8: Competition
- Barnaamijyada caalamiga ah ee miisaaniyaddu waa kuwo adag oo aan ku habboonayn baahida maxalliga ah. SpendWise wuxuu kaga duwan yahay fududeyntiisa, naqshadda responsive-ka ah ee degdegga ah, iyo isticmaalka ku habboon suuqa maxalliga ah.

## Slide 9: Team
- **Asaasaha & Soo-saaraha:** Abdikariim Abdiaziiz Mohamed - Horumariyaha Software-ka oo khibrad u leh Python, HTML/CSS/JavaScript, iyo Maareynta Xogta (MySQL & Git version control).

## Slide 10: Financial Projections
- Sannadka 1aad: Gaaritaanka 1,000 isticmaale oo firfircoon iyo tijaabinta Premium plan-ka.
- Sannadka 2aad: Kordhinta isticmaalayaasha ilaa 10,000 iyo ku darida sifooyin ganacsi oo dheeraad ah.
- Sannadka 3aad: Ballaarinta suuqa iyo helitaanka maalgashi yar.

## Slide 11: Current Status & Milestones
- Hadda waxaan dhisnay naqshaddii webka (UI/UX shell), qaybta xisaabinta JavaScript, waxaanuna ku keydinay koodka GitHub (repository-ga `startup-pitch-deck`). Waxaa naga xiga inaan tijaabino app-ka oo aan suuqa la soo galno.

## Slide 12: The Ask / Conclusion
- Waxaan ka raadineynaa taageero, talooyin xagga ganacsiga ah, iyo fursado aan ku horumarino SpendWise si uu u gaaro kumanaan qof oo u baahan xal maaliyadeed oo fudud. Mahadsanid!
